IfcWorkstationSpace

Definition from IAI: A unit of an office working space. A workstation may contain furniture elements such as work surfaces, file storage, full size panels, and desk surfaces. It can also have freestanding furniture such as desks, chairs, and bookshelves. Office equipment such as fixture lighting and computers, can be included in a workstation.

HISTORY: New Entity in IFC Release 2.0, renamed from IfcWorkstation in IFC Release 2x
ISSUE: See issue log for chances made in IFC Release 2x.

EXPRESS specification:

ENTITY IfcWorkstationSpace
SUBTYPE OF ( IfcSpace);
CirculationRatio  :  OPTIONAL IfcRatioMeasure;
END_ENTITY;

Attribute definitions:

CirculationRatio  :  Ratio of the circulation area within a workstation space to the footprint area of the workstation.
