IfcElectricalApplianceTypeEnum

Definition from IAI: This enumeration defines the different types of electrical appliances an IfcElectricalAppliance object can fulfill:

  • COMPUTER: Computer electrical appliance
  • COPIER: Copier electrical appliance
  • FACSIMILE: Facsimile electrical appliance
  • PRINTER: Printer electrical appliance
  • RADIANTHEATER: Radiant heater
  • TELEPHONE: Telephone electrical appliance
  • USERDEFINED: User-defined electrical appliance
  • NOTDEFINED: Undefined electrical appliance

HISTORY: New enumeration in IFC R2.0. Renamed from IfcElectricalFixtureTypeEnum in IFC R2x
ISSUES: See issues and change log for changes made in IFC R2x.

TYPE IfcElectricalApplianceTypeEnum = ENUMERATION OF
( COMPUTER,
COPIER,
FACSIMILE,
PRINTER,
RADIANTHEATER,
TELEPHONE,
USERDEFINED,
NOTDEFINED);
END_TYPE;
