IfcFanRotationEnum

Definition from IAI: The fan wheel rotation (primarily applicable to centrifugal fans only). The IfcFanRotationEnum contains the following:

  • CLOCKWISE: Clockwise.
  • COUNTERCLOCKWISE: Counter-clockwise.
  • OTHER: Other type of fan rotation.
  • NOTKNOWN: Unknown fan rotation.
  • UNSET: Unspecified fan rotation.

HISTORY: New enumeration in IFC R2x.
ISSUES: See issues and change log for changes made in IFC R2x.


EXPRESS specification:

TYPE IfcFanRotationEnum = ENUMERATION OF
( CLOCKWISE,
COUNTERCLOCKWISE,
OTHER,
NOTKNOWN,
UNSET);
END_TYPE;
