IfcCoilFlowArrangementEnum

Definition from IAI: Flow arrangement of the coil. The IfcCoilFlowArrangementEnum contains the following:

  • COUNTERFLOW: Counterflow coil arrangement.
  • CROSSFLOW: Crossflow coil arrangement.
  • PARALLELFLOW: Parallel flow coil arrangement.
  • DIRECTEXPANSION: Direct Expansion flow coil arrangement.
  • OTHER: Other type of Coil flow arrangement not defined above.
  • USERDEFINED: User-defined Coil flow arrangement.
  • NOTDEFINED: Undefined Coil flow arrangement.

HISTORY: New enumeration in IFC R2x.
ISSUES: See issues and change log for changes made in IFC R2x.
