IfcThermalLoadTypeEnum

Definition from IAI: Defines the various sources of thermal loads or gains for spaces, derived from various use cases. The IfcThermalLoadTypeEnum contains the following:

  • PEOPLESENSIBLE: Sensible thermal loads from people.
  • PEOPLELATENT: Latent thermal loads from people.
  • LIGHTING: Thermal loads from lighting.
  • EQUIPMENTSENSIBLE: Sensible thermal loads from equipment.
  • EQUIPMENTLATENT: Latent thermal loads from equipment.
  • VENTILATIONINDOORAIR: Thermal loads from ventilation air from inside of the building.
  • VENTILATIONOUTSIDEAIR: Thermal loads from ventilation air from outside of the building.
  • RECIRCULATEDAIR: Thermal loads from recirculated air.
  • EXHAUSTAIR: Thermal loads from exhaust air.
  • AIREXCHANGERATE: Thermal loads from the rate of air exchange.
  • DRYBULBTEMPERATURE: Thermal loads from the dry-bulb temperature.
  • RELATIVEHUMIDITY: Thermal loads from the relative humidity.
  • INFILTRATIONSENSIBLE: Sensible thermal loads from infiltration.
  • INFILTRATIONLATENT: Latent thermal loads from infiltration.
  • OTHER: Thermal loads from a source other than those enumerated here.
  • NOTKNOWN: The type of thermal load is not known.

HISTORY: New enumeration in IFC R2.0.
ISSUES: See issues and change log for changes made in IFC R2x.
