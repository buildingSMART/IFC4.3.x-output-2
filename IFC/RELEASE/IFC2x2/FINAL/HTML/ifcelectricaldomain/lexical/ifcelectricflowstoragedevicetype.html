IfcElectricFlowStorageDeviceType

Definition from IAI: An IfcElectricFlowStorageDeviceType is a device in which electrical energy is stored and from which energy may be progressively released.

HISTORY: New entity in IFC 2x2.

Use Definitions

An IfcElectricFlowStorageDeviceType is a subtype of IfcFlowStorageDeviceType that provides for various devices that store electrical energy.

Usage of IfcElectricFlowStorageDeviceType defines the parameters for one or more occurrences of IfcFlowStorageDevice. Parameters are specified through property sets that are enumerated in the IfcElectricFlowStorageDeviceTypeEnum data type.

Geometry Use Definitions

The geometric representation of an occurrence of an IfcElectricFlowStorageDeviceType (instance of IfcFlowStorageDevice) of a particular type is given by the IfcProductDefinitionShape, allowing multiple geometric representations.
