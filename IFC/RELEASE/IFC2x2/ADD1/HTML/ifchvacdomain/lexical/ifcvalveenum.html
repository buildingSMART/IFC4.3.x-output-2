IfcValveEnum

Definition from IAI: This enumeration identifies different types of valves. Note that this enumeration does not define type-driven parameters.

Valid enumerations are:

  • AUTOMATED: Automated valve.
  • ANGLE: Angle valve.
  • AWWA: AWWA valve.
  • BALL: Ball valve.
  • BUTTERFLY: Butterfly valve.
  • CHECK: Check valve.
  • DIVERTER: Diverter valve.
  • GATE: Gate valve.
  • GLOBE: Globe valve.
  • NACE: Nace valve.
  • NEEDLE: Needle valve.
  • PLUG: Plug valve.
  • PIPELINE: Pipeline valve.
  • SAFETY: Safety valve.
  • THREEWAY: Threeway valve.
  • ULFM: ULFM valve.
  • USERDEFINED: User-defined valve.
  • NOTDEFINED: Undefined valve.

HISTORY: New enumeration in IFC R2.0
ISSUES: See issues and change log for changes made in IFC R2x.
