IfcHeatExchangerTypeEnum

Definition from IAI: Enumeration defining the typical types of HeatExchangers. The IfcHeatExchangerTypeEnum contains the following:

  • PLATE: Plate heat exchanger.
  • SHELLANDTUBE: Shell and Tube heat exchanger.
  • OTHER: Other type of heat exchanger.
  • NOTKNOWN: Unknown heat exchanger type.
  • UNSET: Unset heat exchanger type.

HISTORY: New enumeration in IFC R2x.
ISSUES: See issues and change log for changes made in IFC R2x.


EXPRESS specification:

TYPE IfcHeatExchangerTypeEnum = ENUMERATION OF
( PLATE,
SHELLANDTUBE,
OTHER,
NOTKNOWN,
UNSET);
END_TYPE;
