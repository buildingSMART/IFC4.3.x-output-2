IfcManufacturerInformation

Definition from IAI: An IfcManufacturerInformation defines characteristics of manufactured and assembled products that may be given by the manufacturer of the product.

HISTORY New class in IFC Release 2x.
ISSUES See issue and change log for changes made in IFC Release 2x.

Use Definitions

Manufacturer information is a type of IfcPropertySetDefinition and can be assigned to objects through the IfcRelDefinesByProperties class. Its use is specifically intended for products that are manufactured elements and relates generally to the state in which they are provided rather than the resulting state in which they may occur. For instance, information concerning the shipping weight of a product may be relevant during the delivery period of that product but be less important during its operational phase (other than understanding something about its replacement should it be necessary). Equally, operating weight is of importance during the operational phase but be less so during delivery.

Since all attributes of IfcManufacturerInformation are optional, a rule is applied that at least one of Description, ModelLabel, ModelReference or Manufacturer must be asserted (otherwise an object would be created that had no state other than its identity, histry and an optional label).

The QuantityInformation attribute provides most of the expressive capability of the IfcManufactureInformation class. Through this attribute, a set of information can be provided that can describe much about a product that cannot be otherwise captured through the model and that is not otherwise (or best) available through the use of Property Sets. The IfcPhysicalQuantity provides for information about quantifiable properties including count, length, area, volume and weight. Each quantifiable element can be named by the user thus providing flexibiity in the information provided. (Over time, it is intended that the names used for IfcPhysicalQuantity instances will be taken from a standard data dictionary for the industry such as that proposed by ISO TC59/SC13/WG6 but this is not yet available).

EXPRESS specification:

ENTITY IfcManufacturerInformation
SUBTYPE OF ( IfcPropertySetDefinition);
ModelLabel  :  OPTIONAL IfcLabel;
ModelReference  :  OPTIONAL IfcLabel;
Manufacturer  :  OPTIONAL IfcOrganization;
QuantityInformation  :  OPTIONAL SET [1:?] OF IfcPhysicalQuantity;
WHERE
WR1  :  EXISTS (Description) OR EXISTS (ModelLabel) OR EXISTS (ModelReference) OR EXISTS (Manufacturer);
END_ENTITY;

Attribute definitions:

ModelLabel  :  The model number and/or unit designator assigned by the manufacturer of the manufactured item.
ModelReference  :  The name of the manufactured item as used by the manufacturer.
Manufacturer  :  The organization that manufactured and/or assembled the item.
QuantityInformation  :  Information relating to named properties that can be quantified and that can be passed as part of the manufacturer information data set and that are declared explicitly within the model (rather than through declared Property Sets).

Because element quantities can be named, the members of the set can be determined by the sending user. For instance, information might include:

Shipping weight
Operating weight
Packaging size (as length, width and height or volume)
etc.

Formal Propositions:

WR1  :  Ensures that at least one attribute of Description, ModelLabel, ModelReference or Manufacturer is asserted.
