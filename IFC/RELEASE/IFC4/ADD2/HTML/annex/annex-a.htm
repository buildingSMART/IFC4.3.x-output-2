Annex A

(normative)

Computer interpretable listings

This annex contains a listing of the complete EXPRESS schema combining all definitions of clauses 5, 6, 7, and 8 without comments or other explanatory text. These listings are available in computer-interpretable form that may be parsed by computer.

Official schema publications for this release are at the following URLs:

  • IFC EXPRESS: https://standards.buildingsmart.org/IFC/RELEASE/IFC4/Add2/IFC4_ADD2.exp
  • ifcXML XSD: https://standards.buildingsmart.org/IFC/RELEASE/IFC4/Add2/IFC4_ADD2.xsd
  • ifcXML Config: https://standards.buildingsmart.org/IFC/RELEASE/IFC4/Add2/IFC4_ADD2_config.xml
