7.5.4.50 Pset_FanOccurrence

PSET_OCCURRENCEDRIVEN / IfcFan

Fan occurrence attributes attached to an instance of IfcFan.
IfcFanの値に付け加えられたFan属性

buildingSMART Data Dictionary

PSD-XML

  • DischargeType
    P_ENUMERATEDVALUE / IfcLabel / PEnum_FanDischargeType: DUCT, SCREEN, LOUVER, DAMPER, OTHER, NOTKNOWN, UNSET
    DischargeType: Defines the type of connection at the fan discharge. Duct: Discharge into ductwork. Screen: Discharge into screen outlet. Louver: Discharge into a louver. Damper: Discharge into a damper.
    吐出形式: 送風機出口の接続形式定義 Duct：ダクトへの吐き出し Screen:SCREEN内への吐き出し Louver:ルーバーへの吐き出し
  • ApplicationOfFan
    P_ENUMERATEDVALUE / IfcLabel / PEnum_FanApplicationType: SUPPLYAIR, RETURNAIR, EXHAUSTAIR, COOLINGTOWER, OTHER, NOTKNOWN, UNSET
    ApplicationOfFan: The functional application of the fan. SupplyAir: Supply air fan. ReturnAir: Return air fan. ExhaustAir: Exhaust air fan. Other: Other type of application not defined above.
    Fan用途: Fanの系統 SUPPLYAIR, RETURNAIR, EXHAUSTAIR, COOLINGTOWER, OTHER
  • CoilPosition
    P_ENUMERATEDVALUE / IfcLabel / PEnum_FanCoilPosition: DRAWTHROUGH, BLOWTHROUGH, OTHER, NOTKNOWN, UNSET
    CoilPosition: Defines the relationship between a fan and a coil. DrawThrough: Fan located downstream of the coil. BlowThrough: Fan located upstream of the coil.
    コイル位置: Fanとコイル間の関係定義 DwawThrough:ファンはコイルの下流に位置 BlowThrough:ファンはコイルの上流に位置
  • MotorPosition
    P_ENUMERATEDVALUE / IfcLabel / PEnum_FanMotorPosition: INAIRSTREAM, OUTOFAIRSTREAM, OTHER, NOTKNOWN, UNSET
    MotorPosition: Defines the location of the motor relative to the air stream. InAirStream: Fan motor is in the air stream. OutOfAirStream: Fan motor is out of the air stream.
    モーター位置: 空気流路と相対的なモーターの位置定義 流路内、流路外　その他
  • FanMountingType
    P_ENUMERATEDVALUE / IfcLabel / PEnum_FanMountingType: MANUFACTUREDCURB, FIELDERECTEDCURB, CONCRETEPAD, SUSPENDED, WALLMOUNTED, DUCTMOUNTED, OTHER, NOTKNOWN, UNSET
    FanMountingType: Defines the method of mounting the fan in the building.
    Fan設置タイプ: 建物へのFan設置方法定義 MANUFACTUREDCURB, FIELDERECTEDCURB, CONCRETEPAD, SUSPENDED, WALLMOUNTED, DUCTMOUNTED
  • FractionOfMotorHeatToAirStream
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcNormalisedRatioMeasure
    FractionOfMotorHeatToAirStream: Fraction of the motor heat released into the fluid flow.
    モーター排熱: 流体中にモーター発熱が放出される場合にTRUE
  • ImpellerDiameter
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositiveLengthMeasure
    ImpellerDiameter: Diameter of fan wheel - used to scale performance of geometrically similar fans.
    羽根径: ファンホイールの直径　-　幾何学的に類似したファンの性能を基準化するために使用。
