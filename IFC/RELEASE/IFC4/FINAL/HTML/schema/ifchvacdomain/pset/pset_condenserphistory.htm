7.5.4.21 Pset_CondenserPHistory

PSET_PERFORMANCEDRIVEN / IfcCondenser

Condenser performance history attributes.

buildingSMART Data Dictionary

PSD-XML

  • HeatRejectionRate
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcPowerMeasure
    HeatRejectionRate: Sum of the refrigeration effect and the heat equivalent of the power input to the compressor.
  • ExteriorHeatTransferCoefficient
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcThermalTransmittanceMeasure
    ExteriorHeatTransferCoefficient: Exterior heat transfer coefficient associated with exterior surface area.
  • InteriorHeatTransferCoefficient
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcThermalTransmittanceMeasure
    InteriorHeatTransferCoefficient: Interior heat transfer coefficient associated with interior surface area.
  • RefrigerantFoulingResistance
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcThermalResistanceMeasure
    RefrigerantFoulingResistance: Fouling resistance on the refrigerant side.
  • CondensingTemperature
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcThermodynamicTemperatureMeasure
    CondensingTemperature: Refrigerant condensing temperature.
  • LogarithmicMeanTemperatureDifference
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcThermodynamicTemperatureMeasure
    LogarithmicMeanTemperatureDifference: Logarithmic mean temperature difference between refrigerant and water or air.
  • UAcurves
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries
    UAcurves: UV = f (VExterior, VInterior), UV as a function of interior and exterior fluid flow velocity at the entrance.
  • CompressorCondenserHeatGain
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcThermodynamicTemperatureMeasure
    CompressorCondenserHeatGain: Heat gain between condenser inlet to compressor outlet.
  • CompressorCondenserPressureDrop
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcPressureMeasure
    CompressorCondenserPressureDrop: Pressure drop between condenser inlet and compressor outlet.
  • CondenserMeanVoidFraction
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcNormalisedRatioMeasure
    CondenserMeanVoidFraction: Mean void fraction in condenser.
  • WaterFoulingResistance
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcThermalResistanceMeasure
    WaterFoulingResistance: Fouling resistance on water/air side.
