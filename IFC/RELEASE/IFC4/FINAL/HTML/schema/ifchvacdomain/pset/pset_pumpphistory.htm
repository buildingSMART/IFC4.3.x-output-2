7.5.4.81 Pset_PumpPHistory

PSET_PERFORMANCEDRIVEN / IfcPump

Pump performance history attributes.

buildingSMART Data Dictionary

PSD-XML

  • MechanicalEfficiency
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcNormalisedRatioMeasure
    MechanicalEfficiency: The pumps operational mechanical efficiency.
    機械効率: ポンプの稼動時の機械効率
  • OverallEfficiency
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcNormalisedRatioMeasure
    OverallEfficiency: The pump and motor overall operational efficiency.
    全効率: ポンプとモーターの運用全効率
  • PressureRise
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcPressureMeasure
    PressureRise: The developed pressure.
    昇圧: 上がった圧力
  • RotationSpeed
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcRotationalFrequencyMeasure
    RotationSpeed: Pump rotational speed.
    回転速度: ポンプ回転速度
  • Flowrate
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcVolumetricFlowRateMeasure
    Flowrate: The actual operational fluid flowrate.
    流量: 実際の運用流量
  • Power
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcPowerMeasure
    Power: The actual power consumption of the pump.
    動力: ポンプの実動力消費
