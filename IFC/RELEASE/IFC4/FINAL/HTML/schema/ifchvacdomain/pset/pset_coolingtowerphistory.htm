7.5.4.27 Pset_CoolingTowerPHistory

PSET_PERFORMANCEDRIVEN / IfcCoolingTower

Cooling tower performance history attributes.

buildingSMART Data Dictionary

PSD-XML

  • Capacity
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcPowerMeasure
    Capacity: Cooling tower capacity in terms of heat transfer rate of the cooling tower between air stream and water stream.
  • HeatTransferCoefficient
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries
    HeatTransferCoefficient: Heat transfer coefficient-area product.
  • SumpHeaterPower
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcPowerMeasure
    SumpHeaterPower: Electrical heat power of sump heater.
  • UACurve
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries
    UACurve: UA value as a function of fan speed at certain water flow rate, UA = f ( fan speed).
  • Performance
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries
    Performance: Water temperature change as a function of wet-bulb temperature, water entering temperature, water flow rate, air flow rate, Tdiff = f ( Twet-bulb, Twater,in, mwater, mair).
