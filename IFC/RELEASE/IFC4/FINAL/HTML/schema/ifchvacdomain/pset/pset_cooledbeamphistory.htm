7.5.4.23 Pset_CooledBeamPHistory

PSET_PERFORMANCEDRIVEN / IfcCooledBeam

Definition from IAI: Common performance history attributes for a cooled beam.

buildingSMART Data Dictionary

PSD-XML

  • TotalCoolingCapacity
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcPowerMeasure
    TotalCoolingCapacity: Total cooling capacity. This includes cooling capacity of beam and cooling capacity of supply air.
  • TotalHeatingCapacity
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcPowerMeasure
    TotalHeatingCapacity: Total heating capacity. This includes heating capacity of beam and heating capacity of supply air.
  • BeamCoolingCapacity
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcPowerMeasure
    BeamCoolingCapacity: Cooling capacity of beam. This excludes cooling capacity of supply air.
  • BeamHeatingCapacity
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcPowerMeasure
    BeamHeatingCapacity: Heating capacity of beam. This excludes heating capacity of supply air.
  • CoolingWaterFlowRate
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcVolumetricFlowRateMeasure
    CoolingWaterFlowRate: Water flow rate for cooling.
  • HeatingWaterFlowRate
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcVolumetricFlowRateMeasure
    HeatingWaterFlowRate: Water flow rate for heating.
  • CorrectionFactorForCooling
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcReal
    CorrectionFactorForCooling: Correction factor k as a function of water flow rate (used to calculate cooling capacity).
  • CorrectionFactorForHeating
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcReal
    CorrectionFactorForHeating: Correction factor k as a function of water flow rate (used to calculate heating capacity).
  • WaterPressureDropCurves
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcPressureMeasure
    WaterPressureDropCurves: Water pressure drop as function of water flow rate.
  • SupplyWaterTemperatureCooling
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcThermodynamicTemperatureMeasure
    SupplyWaterTemperatureCooling: Supply water temperature in cooling mode.
  • ReturnWaterTemperatureCooling
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcThermodynamicTemperatureMeasure
    ReturnWaterTemperatureCooling: Return water temperature in cooling mode.
  • SupplyWaterTemperatureHeating
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcThermodynamicTemperatureMeasure
    SupplyWaterTemperatureHeating: Supply water temperature in heating mode.
  • ReturnWaterTemperatureHeating
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcTimeSeries / IfcThermodynamicTemperatureMeasure
    ReturnWaterTemperatureHeating: Return water temperature in heating mode.
