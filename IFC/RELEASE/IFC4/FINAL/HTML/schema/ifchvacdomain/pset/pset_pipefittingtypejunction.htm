7.5.4.74 Pset_PipeFittingTypeJunction

PSET_TYPEDRIVENOVERRIDE / IfcPipeFitting / JUNCTION

Pipe fitting type attributes for junction shapes.

buildingSMART Data Dictionary

PSD-XML

  • JunctionType
    P_ENUMERATEDVALUE / IfcLabel / PEnum_PipeFittingJunctionType: TEE, CROSS, OTHER, NOTKNOWN, UNSET
    JunctionType: The type of junction. TEE=3 ports, CROSS = 4 ports.
    継ぎ手タイプ: 接合のタイプ　(T継ぎ手は3本　十字継ぎ手は4本)
  • JunctionLeftAngle
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositivePlaneAngleMeasure
    JunctionLeftAngle: The change of direction of flow for the left junction.
    左継ぎ手角度: 左継ぎ手の流れの方向の変化
  • JunctionLeftRadius
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositiveLengthMeasure
    JunctionLeftRadius: The radius of bending for the left junction.
    左継ぎ手半径: 左継ぎ手の曲がりの半径
  • JunctionRightAngle
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositivePlaneAngleMeasure
    JunctionRightAngle: The change of direction of flow for the right junction where 0 indicates straight segment.
    右継ぎ手角度: 右継ぎ手の流れの方向の変化
  • JunctionRightRadius
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositiveLengthMeasure
    JunctionRightRadius: The radius of bending for the right junction where 0 indicates sharp bend.
    右継ぎ手半径: 右継ぎ手の曲がりの半径(ゼロは鋭い曲がりを意味する)
