7.8.5.2 Qto_PileBaseQuantities

QTO_TYPEDRIVENOVERRIDE / IfcPile

Base quantities that are common to the definition of all occurrences of piles.

QTO-XML

  • Length
    Q_LENGTH
    Length: Total length of the pile not taking into account any cut-out's or other processing features.
  • CrossSectionArea
    Q_AREA
    CrossSectionArea: Total area of the cross section (or profile) of the pile.
  • OuterSurfaceArea
    Q_AREA
    OuterSurfaceArea: Total area of the extruded surfaces of the pile (not taking into account the end cap areas), normally generated as perimeter * length.
  • GrossSurfaceArea
    Q_AREA
    GrossSurfaceArea: Total area of the pile, normally generated as perimeter * length + 2 * cross section area. It is the sum of OuterSurfaceArea + (2 x CrossSectionArea) and shall only be given, if the OuterSurfaceArea and CrossSectionArea cannot be established separately.
  • GrossVolume
    Q_VOLUME
    GrossVolume: Total gross volume of the pile, not taking into account possible processing features (cut-out's, etc.) or openings and recesses.
  • NetVolume
    Q_VOLUME
    NetVolume: Total net volume of the pile, taking into account possible processing features (cut-out's, etc.) or openings and recesses.
  • GrossWeight
    Q_WEIGHT
    GrossWeight: Total gross weight of the pile without add-on parts, not taking into account possible processing features (cut-out's, etc.) or openings and recesses.
  • NetWeight
    Q_WEIGHT
    NetWeight: Total net weight of the pile without add-on parts, taking into account possible processing features (cut-out's, etc.) or openings and recesses.
