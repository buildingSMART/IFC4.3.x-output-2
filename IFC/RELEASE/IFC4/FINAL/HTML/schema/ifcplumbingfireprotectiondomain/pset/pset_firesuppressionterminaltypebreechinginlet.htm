7.6.4.1 Pset_FireSuppressionTerminalTypeBreechingInlet

PSET_TYPEDRIVENOVERRIDE / IfcFireSuppressionTerminal / BREECHINGINLET

Symmetrical pipe fitting that unites two or more inlets into a single pipe (BS6100 330 114 adapted).
ひとつのパイプ（適合BS6100330114）に2つ以上の入口をもつ対称配管継手。
