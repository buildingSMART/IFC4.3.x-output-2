7.7.3.18 IfcStructuralPlanarAction

gleichmäßige Flächenlast

This entity defines an action with constant value which is distributed over a surface.

HISTORY  New entity in IFC2x2.
IFC4 CHANGE  Intermediate supertype IfcStructuralSurfaceAction inserted.
NOTE  Like its supertype IfcStructuralSurfaceAction, this action type may also act on curved faces.

 <xs:complexType name="IfcStructuralPlanarAction">
  <xs:complexContent>

EXPRESS Specification:

ENTITY IfcStructuralPlanarAction
SUBTYPE OF IfcStructuralSurfaceAction;
WHERE
SuitableLoadType : SIZEOF(['IFCSTRUCTURALLOADRESOURCE.IFCSTRUCTURALLOADPLANARFORCE', 'IFCSTRUCTURALLOADRESOURCE.IFCSTRUCTURALLOADTEMPERATURE'] * TYPEOF(SELF\IfcStructuralActivity.AppliedLoad)) = 1;
ConstPredefinedType : SELF\IfcStructuralSurfaceAction.PredefinedType = IfcStructuralSurfaceActivityTypeEnum.CONST;
END_ENTITY;

 EXPRESS-G diagram

Formal Propositions:

SuitableLoadType : A planar action shall place either a planar force or a temperature load.
ConstPredefinedType : This surface action subtype is restricted to constant load distribution over its domain.
