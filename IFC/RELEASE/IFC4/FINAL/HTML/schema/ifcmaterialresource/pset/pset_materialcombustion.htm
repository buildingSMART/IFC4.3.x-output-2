8.10.5.1 Pset_MaterialCombustion

PSET_TYPEDRIVENOVERRIDE / IfcMaterial

A set of extended material properties of products of combustion generated by elements typically used within the context of building services and flow distribution systems.

buildingSMART Data Dictionary

PSD-XML

  • SpecificHeatCapacity
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcSpecificHeatCapacityMeasure
    SpecificHeatCapacity: Specific heat of the products of combustion: heat energy absorbed per temperature unit.
  • N20Content
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositiveRatioMeasure
    N20Content: Nitrous oxide (N2O) content of the products of combustion. This is measured in weight of N2O per unit weight and is therefore unitless.
  • COContent
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositiveRatioMeasure
    COContent: Carbon monoxide (CO) content of the products of combustion. This is measured in weight of CO per unit weight and is therefore unitless.
  • CO2Content
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositiveRatioMeasure
    CO2Content: Carbon dioxide (CO2) content of the products of combustion. This is measured in weight of CO2 per unit weight and is therefore unitless.
