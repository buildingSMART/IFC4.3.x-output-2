8.10.5.6 Pset_MaterialHygroscopic

PSET_TYPEDRIVENOVERRIDE / IfcMaterial

A set of hygroscopic properties of materials.

buildingSMART Data Dictionary

PSD-XML

  • UpperVaporResistanceFactor
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositiveRatioMeasure
    UpperVaporResistanceFactor: The vapor permeability relationship of air/material (typically value > 1), measured in high relative humidity (typically in 95/50 % RH).
  • LowerVaporResistanceFactor
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositiveRatioMeasure
    LowerVaporResistanceFactor: The vapor permeability relationship of air/material (typically value > 1), measured in low relative humidity (typically in 0/50 % RH).
  • IsothermalMoistureCapacity
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcIsothermalMoistureCapacityMeasure
    IsothermalMoistureCapacity: Based on water vapor density.
  • VaporPermeability
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcVaporPermeabilityMeasure
    VaporPermeability: The rate of water vapor transmission per unit area per unit of vapor pressure differential under test conditions.
  • MoistureDiffusivity
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcMoistureDiffusivityMeasure
    MoistureDiffusivity: Moisture diffusivity is a transport property that is frequently used in the hygrothermal analysis of building envelope components.
