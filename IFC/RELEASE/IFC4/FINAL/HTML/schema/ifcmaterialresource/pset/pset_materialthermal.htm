8.10.5.10 Pset_MaterialThermal

PSET_TYPEDRIVENOVERRIDE / IfcMaterial

A set of thermal material properties.

buildingSMART Data Dictionary

PSD-XML

  • SpecificHeatCapacity
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcSpecificHeatCapacityMeasure
    SpecificHeatCapacity: Defines the specific heat of the material: heat energy absorbed per temperature unit.
  • BoilingPoint
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcThermodynamicTemperatureMeasure
    BoilingPoint: The boiling point of the material (fluid).
  • FreezingPoint
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcThermodynamicTemperatureMeasure
    FreezingPoint: The freezing point of the material (fluid).
  • ThermalConductivity
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcThermalConductivityMeasure
    ThermalConductivity: The rate at which thermal energy is transmitted through the material.
