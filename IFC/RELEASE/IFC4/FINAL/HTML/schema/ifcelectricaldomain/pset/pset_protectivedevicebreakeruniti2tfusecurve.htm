7.4.4.44 Pset_ProtectiveDeviceBreakerUnitI2TFuseCurve

PSET_TYPEDRIVENOVERRIDE / IfcProtectiveDevice

A coherent set of attributes representing curves for melting- and breaking-energy of a fuse. Note - A fuse may be associated with different instances of this property set providing information related to different basic characteristics.

(2) Defined value: MeltingEnergy: A list of minimum 2 and maximum 8 numbers providing the energy whereby the fuse is starting to melt, I2t, in [A2s] for points in the current/melting_energy log/log coordinate space.
