7.1.2.1 IfcDoorPanelOperationEnum

This enumeration defines the basic ways how individual door panels operate as shown in Figure 249.

HISTORY  New enumeration in IFC2.0.
IFC4 CHANGE  Enumerator FIXEDPANEL added.

Enumerated Item Definitions:

Figure 249 — Door operations

The opening direction of the door panels is given by the local placement of the IfcDoor. The positive y-axis determines the direction as shown in Figure 157.
