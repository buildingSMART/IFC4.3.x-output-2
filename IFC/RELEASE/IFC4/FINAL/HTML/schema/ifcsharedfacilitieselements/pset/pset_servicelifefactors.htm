6.4.4.13 Pset_ServiceLifeFactors

PSET_OCCURRENCEDRIVEN / IfcSystem

Captures various factors that impact the expected service life of elements within the system or zone.

buildingSMART Data Dictionary

PSD-XML

  • QualityOfComponents
    P_BOUNDEDVALUE / IfcPositiveRatioMeasure
    QualityOfComponents: Adjustment of the service life resulting from the effect of the quality of components used.
  • DesignLevel
    P_BOUNDEDVALUE / IfcPositiveRatioMeasure
    DesignLevel: Adjustment of the service life resulting from the effect of design level employed.
  • WorkExecutionLevel
    P_BOUNDEDVALUE / IfcPositiveRatioMeasure
    WorkExecutionLevel: Adjustment of the service life resulting from the effect of the quality of work executed.
  • IndoorEnvironment
    P_BOUNDEDVALUE / IfcPositiveRatioMeasure
    IndoorEnvironment: Adjustment of the service life resulting from the effect of the indoor environment (where appropriate).
  • OutdoorEnvironment
    P_BOUNDEDVALUE / IfcPositiveRatioMeasure
    OutdoorEnvironment: Adjustment of the service life resulting from the effect of the outdoor environment (where appropriate)
  • InUseConditions
    P_BOUNDEDVALUE / IfcPositiveRatioMeasure
    InUseConditions: Adjustment of the service life resulting from the effect of the conditions in which components are operating.
  • MaintenanceLevel
    P_BOUNDEDVALUE / IfcPositiveRatioMeasure
    MaintenanceLevel: Adjustment of the service life resulting from the effect of the level or degree of maintenance applied to dcomponents.
