6.4.4.17 Pset_Warranty

PSET_TYPEDRIVENOVERRIDE / IfcElement

An assurance given by the seller or provider of an artefact that the artefact is without defects and will operate as described for a defined period of time without failure and that if a defect does arise during that time, that it will be corrected by the seller or provider.

buildingSMART Data Dictionary

PSD-XML

  • WarrantyIdentifier
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcIdentifier
    WarrantyIdentifier: The identifier assigned to a warranty.
  • WarrantyStartDate
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcDate
    WarrantyStartDate: The date on which the warranty commences.
  • WarrantyEndDate
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcDate
    WarrantyEndDate: The date on which the warranty expires.
  • IsExtendedWarranty
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcBoolean
    IsExtendedWarranty: Indication of whether this is an extended warranty whose duration is greater than that normally assigned to an artefact (=TRUE) or not (= FALSE).
  • WarrantyPeriod
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcTimeMeasure
    WarrantyPeriod: The time duration during which a manufacturer or supplier guarantees or warrants the performance of an artefact.
  • PointOfContact
    P_REFERENCEVALUE / IfcOrganization
    PointOfContact: The organization that should be contacted for action under the terms of the warranty. Note that the role of the organization (manufacturer, supplier, installer etc.) is determined by the IfcActorRole attribute of IfcOrganization.
  • WarrantyContent
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcText
    WarrantyContent: The content of the warranty.
  • Exclusions
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcText
    Exclusions: Items, conditions or actions that may be excluded from the warranty or that may cause the warranty to become void.
