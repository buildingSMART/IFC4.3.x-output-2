6.4.4.12 Pset_ServiceLife

PSET_TYPEDRIVENOVERRIDE / IfcElement

Captures the period of time that an artifact will last. HISTORY: Introduced in IFC2X4 as replacement for IfcServiceLife.

buildingSMART Data Dictionary

PSD-XML

  • ServiceLifeDuration
    P_BOUNDEDVALUE / IfcDuration
    ServiceLifeDuration: The length or duration of a service life. The lower bound indicates pessimistic service life, the upper bound indicates optimistic service life, and the setpoint indicates the typical service life.
  • MeanTimeBetweenFailure
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcDuration
    MeanTimeBetweenFailure: The average time duration between instances of failure of a product.
