6.2.3.14 IfcFlowFitting

Raccord d'installation fluides
Verbinder / Formstück (allgemein)

The distribution flow element IfcFlowFitting defines the occurrence of a junction or transition in a flow distribution system, such as an elbow or tee. Its type is defined by IfcFlowFittingType or its subtypes.

HISTORY  New entity in IFC2.0.
IFC4 CHANGE  This entity has been deprecated for instantiation and will become ABSTRACT in a future release; new subtypes should now be used instead.

ENTITY IfcFlowFitting
SUPERTYPE OF(ONEOF(IfcCableCarrierFitting, IfcCableFitting, IfcDuctFitting, IfcJunctionBox, IfcPipeFitting))

END_ENTITY;
