6.2.4.4 Pset_DistributionChamberElementTypeInspectionChamber

PSET_TYPEDRIVENOVERRIDE / IfcDistributionChamberElement / INSPECTIONCHAMBER

Chamber constructed on a drain, sewer or pipeline and with a removable cover, that permits visible inspection.
排水、下水管の上にある・点検用可移動カバー付けパイプスペース。
