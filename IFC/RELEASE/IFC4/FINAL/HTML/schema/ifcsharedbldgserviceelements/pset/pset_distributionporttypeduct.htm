6.2.4.16 Pset_DistributionPortTypeDuct

PSET_TYPEDRIVENOVERRIDE / IfcDistributionPort / AIRCONDITIONING

Duct port occurrence attributes attached to an instance of IfcDistributionPort.

  • ConnectionType
    P_ENUMERATEDVALUE / IfcLabel / PEnum_DuctConnectionType: BEADEDSLEEVE, COMPRESSION, CRIMP, DRAWBAND, DRIVESLIP, FLANGED, OUTSIDESLEEVE, SLIPON, SOLDERED, SSLIP, STANDINGSEAM, SWEDGE, WELDED, OTHER, NONE, USERDEFINED, NOTDEFINED
    ConnectionType: The end-style treatment of the duct port: BEADEDSLEEVE: Beaded Sleeve. COMPRESSION: Compression. CRIMP: Crimp. DRAWBAND: Drawband. DRIVESLIP: Drive slip. FLANGED: Flanged. OUTSIDESLEEVE: Outside Sleeve. SLIPON: Slipon. SOLDERED: Soldered. SSLIP: S-Slip. STANDINGSEAM: Standing seam. SWEDGE: Swedge. WELDED: Welded. OTHER: Another type of end-style has been applied. NONE: No end-style has been applied.
