8.15.4.3 Pset_ProfileMechanical

PSET_TYPEDRIVENOVERRIDE / IfcProfileDef

This is a collection of mechanical properties that are applicable to virtually all profile classes. Most of these properties are especially used in structural analysis.

  • MassPerLength
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcMassPerLengthMeasure
    MassPerLength: Mass per length, i.e. mass of a beam with a unit length of extrusion. For example measured in kg/m.
  • CrossSectionArea
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcAreaMeasure
    CrossSectionArea: Area of the profile. For example measured in mm2. If given, the value of the cross section area shall be greater than zero.
  • Perimeter
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositiveLengthMeasure
    Perimeter: Perimeter of the profile for calculating the surface area. For example measured in mm.
  • MinimumPlateThickness
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositiveLengthMeasure
    MinimumPlateThickness: This value may be needed for stress analysis and to handle buckling problems. It can also be derived from the given profile geometry or classification and therefore it is only an optional feature allowing for an explicit description. For example measured in mm.
  • MaximumPlateThickness
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositiveLengthMeasure
    MaximumPlateThickness: This value may be needed for stress analysis and to handle buckling problems. It can also be derived from the given profile geometry or classification and therefore it is only an optional feature allowing for an explicit description. For example measured in mm.
  • CentreOfGravityInX
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcLengthMeasure
    CentreOfGravityInX: Location of the profile's centre of gravity (geometric centroid), measured along xp.
  • CentreOfGravityInY
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcLengthMeasure
    CentreOfGravityInY: Location of the profile's centre of gravity (geometric centroid), measured along yp.
  • ShearCentreZ
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcLengthMeasure
    ShearCentreZ: Location of the profile's shear centre, measured along zs.
  • ShearCentreY
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcLengthMeasure
    ShearCentreY: Location of the profile's shear centre, measured along ys.
  • MomentOfInertiaY
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcMomentOfInertiaMeasure
    MomentOfInertiaY: Moment of inertia about ys (second moment of area, about ys). For example measured in mm4.
  • MomentOfInertiaZ
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcMomentOfInertiaMeasure
    MomentOfInertiaZ: Moment of inertia about zs (second moment of area, about zs). For example measured in mm4
  • MomentOfInertiaYZ
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcMomentOfInertiaMeasure
    MomentOfInertiaYZ: Moment of inertia about ys and zs (product moment of area). For example measured in mm4.
  • TorsionalConstantX
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcMomentOfInertiaMeasure
    TorsionalConstantX: Torsional constant about xs. For example measured in mm4.
  • WarpingConstant
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcWarpingConstantMeasure
    WarpingConstant: Warping constant of the profile for torsional action. For example measured in mm6.
  • ShearDeformationAreaZ
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcAreaMeasure
    ShearDeformationAreaZ: Area of the profile for calculating the shear deformation due to a shear force parallel to zs. For example measured in mm². If given, the shear deformation area zs shall be non-negative.
  • ShearDeformationAreaY
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcAreaMeasure
    ShearDeformationAreaY: Area of the profile for calculating the shear deformation due to a shear force parallel to ys. For example measured in mm². If given, the shear deformation area ys shall be non-negative.
  • MaximumSectionModulusY
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcSectionModulusMeasure
    MaximumSectionModulusY: Bending resistance about the ys axis at the point with maximum zs ordinate. For example measured in mm³.
  • MinimumSectionModulusY
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcSectionModulusMeasure
    MinimumSectionModulusY: Bending resistance about the ys axis at the point with minimum zs ordinate. For example measured in mm³.
  • MaximumSectionModulusZ
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcSectionModulusMeasure
    MaximumSectionModulusZ: Bending resistance about the zs axis at the point with maximum ys ordinate. For example measured in mm³.
  • MinimumSectionModulusZ
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcSectionModulusMeasure
    MinimumSectionModulusZ: Bending resistance about the zs axis at the point with minimum ys ordinate. For example measured in mm³.
  • TorsionalSectionModulus
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcSectionModulusMeasure
    TorsionalSectionModulus: Torsional resistance (about xs). For example measured in mm³.
  • ShearAreaZ
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcAreaMeasure
    ShearAreaZ: Area of the profile for calculating the shear stress due to shear force parallel to the section analysis axis zs. For example measured in mm². If given, the shear area zs shall be non-negative.
  • ShearAreaY
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcAreaMeasure
    ShearAreaY: Area of the profile for calculating the shear stress due to shear force parallel to the section analysis axis ys. For example measured in mm². If given, the shear area ys shall be non-negative.
  • PlasticShapeFactorY
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositiveRatioMeasure
    PlasticShapeFactorY: Ratio of plastic versus elastic bending moment capacity about the section analysis axis ys. A dimensionless value.
  • PlasticShapeFactorZ
    P_SINGLEVALUE / IfcPositiveRatioMeasure
    PlasticShapeFactorZ: Ratio of plastic versus elastic bending moment capacity about the section analysis axis zs. A dimensionless value.
