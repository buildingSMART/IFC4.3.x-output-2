7.3.5.2 Qto_ConstructionMaterialResourceBaseQuantities

QTO_TYPEDRIVENOVERRIDE / IfcConstructionMaterialResource

Base quantities that are common to the definition of all occurrences of construction material resources.

QTO-XML

  • GrossVolume
    Q_VOLUME
    GrossVolume: Total gross volume of the material, including material placed and wasted.
  • NetVolume
    Q_VOLUME
    NetVolume: Total net volume of the material, including material placed but excluding material wasted.
  • GrossWeight
    Q_WEIGHT
    GrossWeight: Total gross weight of the material, including material placed and wasted.
  • NetWeight
    Q_WEIGHT
    NetWeight: Total net weight of the material, including material placed but excluding material wasted.
